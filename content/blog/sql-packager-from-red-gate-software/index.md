<p><a href="http://www.red-gate.com" target="none" rel="noopener">Red Gate</a> just announced their <a href="http://www.red-gate.com/sql/sql_packager.htm" target="none" rel="noopener">SQL Packager</a> product, which makes packing up and deploying databases a snap. You can package structure, data, or both. It creates a .NET executable which you can walk over to your target machine and double-click. Wizards and templates ensure it appeals to the broad range of SQL professionals.</p>
<p>... just another wonderful product from the company that brought us SQL Compare and ANTS.</p>
