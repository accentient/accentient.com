<p>I've seen Test Driven Development work, so why not adjust it slightly and have the attendees (who own the requirements after all) drive the presentation? Today, at the <a href="http://www.devfish.net/articles/inbetween/default.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">in-between conference</a> (a.k.a. Microsoft Community Summit 2008), I did just that. I had the attendees drive my four hour presentation. I did this in the <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Space_Technology" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open Space</a> room, and it not only fit with the theme of that room, but it worked great!</p> <p>As the attendees arrived, I handed them 3-5 3x5 cards - the <a href="http://www.3m.com/us/office/postit/products/prod_cards_sort.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">cool ones from 3M</a> that you can sort, stack, and stick to surfaces.</p> <p>Here are the topics (backlog items) that they came up with:</p> <ul> <li><em>How do you customize work item types? </em> <li><em>(What) team size to justify the usage of Team System? </em> <li><em>What's new and improved in VSTS 2008 vs. VSTS 2005? </em> <li><em>Continuous Integration (x 3) </em> <li><em>What performance degradation (can occur) from extensive branching? </em> <li><em>Integration with external tools (e.g. Mercury Quality Center, Doors) </em> <li><em>TDD </em> <li><em>Multiple builds running at the same time </em> <li><em>How to limit CI build to only trigger when for certain check-ins (by location) </em> <li><em>Best practices </em> <li><em>How to customize Code Analysis </em> <li><em>What makes VSTS more beneficial than VS Professional? </em> <li><em>What is Team Foundation Server?</em></li></ul> <p>And my personal favorite:</p> <ul> <li><em>I'm here to see if you're a good presenter because my company is thinking of bringing you in for a day to teach the team.</em></li></ul> <p>For those of you who attended my talk, here's a link to my <a href="CommunitySummit-VSTSNotes.txt" target="_blank" rel="noopener">notes</a> and my <a href="CommunitySummit-VSTSWorstPractices.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">worst practices presentation</a>.</p>
