<p>As a software developer, I've often wrestled with the decision of how much to price my creation. I remember watching my grandfather create a large, beautiful watercolor painting and then stick a price tag of $10,000 on it. When asked how he could justify $10k in 3 hours his answer was it took him 40 years + 3 hours. That makes sense.</p>
<p>It would be nice if&nbsp;this would apply to software development, because I'd be at 20 years mark!. Since it doesn't work that way, try reading <a href="http://msdn.microsoft.com/library/default.asp?url=/library/en-us/dnsoftware/html/software08052004.asp" target="none" rel="noopener">this article</a> on how to compute a price, all the time keeping your revenue in mind.</p>
