<p>Not sure where this list came from, but a few of them hit close to home:</p>
<ol>
<li>You work very odd hours.</li>
<li>You are paid a lot of money to keep your client happy.</li>
<li>You are paid well but your pimp gets most of the money.</li>
<li>You spend a majority of your time in a hotel room.</li>
<li>You charge by the hour but your time can be extended.</li>
<li>Creating fantasies for your clients is rewarded.</li>
<li>It's difficult to have a family.</li>
<li>If a client beats you up, the pimp just sends you to another client.</li>
<li>People ask you, "What do you do?" and you can't explain it.</li>
<li>Your client pays for your hotel room plus your hourly rate.</li>
<li>Your client always wants to know how much you charge and what they get for the money.</li>
<li>You know the pimp is charging more than you are worth but if the client is foolish enough to pay it's not your problem.</li>
<li>You are rated on your "performance" in an excruciating ordeal.</li>
<li>The client always thinks your "cut" of your billing rate is higher than it actually is, and in turn, expects miracles from you.</li>
<li>When you deduct your "take" from your billing rate, you constantly wonder if you could get a better deal with another pimp.</li></ol>
