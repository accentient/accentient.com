<p>More fun with Vista.&nbsp; I love the OS, and there's so much to recommend it, but I keep running into WEIRD problems!&nbsp; Here's another one...&nbsp; I like to listen to <a href="http://www.dotnetrocks.com">DotNetRocks</a>, <a href="http://www.hanselminutes.com">Hanselminutes</a>, and other podcasts during my commute to and from client sites.&nbsp; They offer downloads in both WMA and MP3 formats.&nbsp; I've generally used MP3, but thought, "Hey, I should switch to WMA, since that's native to Windows Media.&nbsp; Unfortunately, when I tried to drag any WMA files onto Windows Media 11 in Vista, it won't allow me to drop them onto the 'To Burn List'2013-08-28 13:41:29', or add them any other way I was able to see.&nbsp; However, MP3 files can be dragged onto the burn list easily.&nbsp; Weird.&nbsp; So, I guess it's back to MP3s.&nbsp; </p>
<p>NOTE:&nbsp; For those of you writing to tell me what I'm doing wrong and how to fix it so that it works, I appreciate it.&nbsp; However, my main complaint isn't that I can't spend 30 minutes (or even 10) trying to figure it out and making it work, it's taht Vista/WM11 seems to choke on native files, but easily support MP3s.</p>
