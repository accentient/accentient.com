<p>Some changes this year from Microsoft. It seems that they are merging the&nbsp;usually separate and distinct Tech-Ed Europe (traditional audience of&nbsp;50% devs/50% IT pros) and Microsoft IT Forum (100% IT pros) into just "Tech Ed Europe". They will be run in two consecutive weeks in November:</p>
<ul>
<li>Tech Ed: Developers (week November 6th, CCIB Barcelona, Spain)
<li>Tech Ed: IT Forum (week November 13th, CCIB Barcelona, Spain)</li></ul>
<p>For more information, check out the <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/europe/teched" target="none" rel="noopener">Tech-Ed Europe site</a>.</p>
