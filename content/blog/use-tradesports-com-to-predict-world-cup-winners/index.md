<p>I remember two years ago, <a href="http://www.teamsystem.info/" target="none" rel="noopener">Steven Borg</a>, told me about <a href="http://www.tradesports.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">TradeSports</a>, an Irish Website where you can trade "futures" on just about anything, such as election results, sporting events, entertainment, and even when the bird flu will hit the US (<a href="http://www.tradesports.com/aav2/trading/tradingHTML.jsp?evID=36620&amp;eventSelect=36620&amp;updateList=true" target="none" rel="noopener">no kidding</a>). I believe Steve even made some money on the 2004 presidential election using TradeSports.</p>
<p>Well, naturally, with the largest sporting event in the world starting tomorrow, I checked them out, and they've got exchanges setup by group, by game, overall index, by scorer, and by outright ranking at the end of the tournament. So, if you are looking for extremely accurate predictors, check them out. If you want to trade, be sure to read up on them. You can find more about TradeSports <a href="http://www.tradesports.com/aav2/aboutUs.jsp" target="none" rel="noopener">here</a>.</p>
