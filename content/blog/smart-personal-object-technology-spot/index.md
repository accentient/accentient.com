<p>Here's a good <a href="http://www.sys-con.com/story/?storyid=46614&amp;DE=1" target="none" rel="noopener">introductory article</a> on SPOT, written by the product team.</p>
<p>As a reminder,&nbsp;Smart Personal Objects are everyday objects, such as clocks, pens, key-chains, billfolds, and <a href="http://direct.msn.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">wristwatches</a>&nbsp;that are made smarter, more personalized and more useful through the use of special software.</p>
