While teaching my C# students about passing command-line parameters to console applications, I remember a favorite command line utility of mine from days gone past. More importantly, I remember meeting the man who invented this popular compression utility. His name was Phil Katz and I had the opportunity to meet this cultural icon at ComDex in 1991.

&nbsp;

Sadly though, he died at a young age of 37. You can read the story <a href="http://www.bbsdocumentary.com/research/CONTROVERSY/LAWSUITS/SEA/pkzip.htm" target="none" rel="noopener">here</a>.

&nbsp;

By the way, he invented <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKZIP">PKZIP</a>, if you ever heard of it.
