<p>Code camp is this month!&nbsp; It comes to the&nbsp;DeVry campus on October 22nd and 23rd.&nbsp; Yep!&nbsp; That's a weekend, and that means you don't have to ask for time off work to attend.&nbsp; </p>
<p><img height=90 src="http://pacwest.ms/codecamp/images/codecamp_sea_badge.gif" width=120 border=0></p>
<p>If you missed the&nbsp;Code Camp&nbsp;in Portland, this is your chance.&nbsp; I attended and spoke there, and it was the best conference I've ever attended, including PDC 2005 and TechEd 2005!&nbsp; It's a great idea.&nbsp; Just coders talking to coders!&nbsp; Every presentation includes CODE, and there's no marketing...&nbsp; And the presenters are all coders.&nbsp; </p>
<p>It's FREE to attend!&nbsp; And, if you'2013-08-28 13:48:43'd like to speak, you can submit an abstract.&nbsp; You don't get paid for speaking, but you do get to talk about something cool!&nbsp; Here's a quick summary.&nbsp; You can read more about it, and register,&nbsp;<a href="http://pacwest.ms/codecamp/sea/1/">here</a>.</p>
<p><strong><font size=3>Code Camp Details</font></strong><br />We are going to have 13 tracks and probably 300+ attendees (we have capacity for ~1000). Portland registered 300 in two weeks.&nbsp; This is our first Camp so we don't really know how many will attend. We are looking for about 80-90 talks for the two days. There will be a big publicity push this week and every week till the start of the camp.</p>
<p><strong>Code Camp is devoted to coders, regardless of platform, OS or favorite programming language.</strong>&nbsp; That means we are encouraging talks in lots of disciplines.&nbsp; We want to cross-pollinate the developers in the area - exposing them to ideas and tools that they may not have seen before.</p>
<p><strong>Code Camp is open for kids and teens too.</strong>&nbsp; At the Portland Camp there were numerous fathers there with their 10-16 year old kids.&nbsp; The younger ones were totally thrilled to be there.</p>
<p><strong>Code Camps encourage new speakers to join in too.</strong> Friday night we will have a gathering for speakers which includes speaker training and the opportunity to talk to seasoned presenters.&nbsp; </p>
<p><strong>Dates: October 22,23, 2005<br /></strong>Times: Daytime<br />BBQ: Saturday Evening<br />Location:&nbsp; DeVrys University -- Federal Way WA<br />Cost: Free<br />Payment to Speakers: None<br />Session Length: Variable but most will be 75 minutes<br />Registration:&nbsp; If you are a speaker you must be registered for the event.</p>
