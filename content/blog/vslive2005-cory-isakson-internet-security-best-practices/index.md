<p><a href="http://blog.coryisakson.com">Cory Isakson</a> is giving a presention on security on the internet.&nbsp; Looking specifically at extranet solutions (and some good ideas in internet solutions as well).&nbsp; About 80 people in attendence.</p>
<p>Threats:</p>
<ul>
<li>Man in the middle
<li>Session reply
<li>Query string manipulation
<li>HTTP header manipulation
<li>Cookie&nbsp;manipulation
<li>Form field manipulation
<li>Luring attacks</li></ul>
<p>Recommendations:</p>
<ul>
<li>Authorization:
<ul>
<li>Access Control Lists
<li>Authorization Manager - ties in with Active Directory credentials
<li><strong>ASP.NET 2.0 Permission Manager</strong> - not MS, but ties in directly to the Provider model.&nbsp; This looks exciting!&nbsp; Cory really covered this well, even giving some real code.&nbsp; Plus, his business logic for why this is important is very compelling.&nbsp; Check this out at Google.&nbsp; Or (I did it for you) <a href="http://www.gotdotnet.com/workspaces/workspace.aspx?id=762350f9-7d40-44ca-8ec0-4655e1a7682b">here</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>Good presentation.&nbsp; Enough code to keep us interested, with enough tips from experience to make us feel like we've had some good experienced-based tricks we can take back to us.</p>
