<p>The GISUG had its&nbsp;inaugural meeting last week.&nbsp;From what Pete <font size=2>Hohenhaus tells me, there&nbsp;</font>was a lot of enthusiasm, participation, and interest. Not including the presenters, they had&nbsp;35 attendees. All in all an excellent beginning&nbsp;for this promising group.</p>
<p>Owen Allen, Jason Mauer, and Chris Barnard presented on Office 2007/SharePoint 2007.&nbsp;Owen and Jason focused many of the new capabilities, as well as the overall&nbsp;look and feel.&nbsp;Chris did an overview of the product release.&nbsp;Pete&nbsp;presented on some perspectives of what SharePoint is, how it can be used, and how best it&nbsp;can assist&nbsp;with collaborative work.</p>
<p>Pete has&nbsp;setup a controlled access <a href="http://groups.msn.com/GreaterIdahoSharePointUsersGroup" target="none" rel="noopener">MSN Group</a> and they will be adding content and sending out invitations to join very soon.</p>
