<p>Once the most secret place in Britain, <a href="http://www.reuters.co.uk/newsPackageArticle.jhtml?type=topNews&amp;storyID=527494&amp;section=news" target="none" rel="noopener">Bletchley Park</a> is now giving the public a first look into the hut where math genius Alan Turing worked on cracking the Nazi's Enigma codes. I would love to visit this, and it turns out that my friend <a href="http://www.sqlreportingservices.net" target="none" rel="noopener">Peter Blackburn</a> lives only about 40 miles from there. Looks like I'll be heading over for a visit soon!</p>
