<p>We've been getting some questions lately on this, and here are the answers:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Partners at the <em>Certified</em> Partner level will receive the new Microsoft Visual Studio 2005 Professional Edition with MSDN Premium Subscription.&nbsp; As before, this will include 5 MSDN licenses.</p>
<li>
<p>Partners at the <em>Gold</em> Certified Partner level will receive the enhanced version of Microsoft Visual Studio 2005 Team Edition for Software Developers with MSDN Premium Subscription.&nbsp;As before, this will include 10 MSDN licenses.</p></li></ul>
<p>Notice that neither level include Architect or Test editions, nor&nbsp;do they include Team Suite. Read <a href="http://forums.microsoft.com/MSDN/ShowPost.aspx?PostID=126616&amp;SiteID=1" target="none" rel="noopener">Ajay's post</a> for more information.</p>
