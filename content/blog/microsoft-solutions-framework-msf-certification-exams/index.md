<p>Microsoft certification exam # 074-100 (<a href="http://msdn.microsoft.com/vstudio/enterprise/msf/default.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">MSF</a> Practitioner Exam) is retiring, July 28th and is being <a href="http://msdn.microsoft.com/vstudio/enterprise/msf/ExamFAQ.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">replaced by exam # 070-301</a>.</p>
<p>For those MCTs who want to continue to deliver MSF courses, such as <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/learning/syllabi/en-us/1846Afinal.mspx" target="none" rel="noopener">Course 1846A</a>, after December 31, 2004,&nbsp;they will be required to pass 070-301 in addition to becoming an MCT with MSF Competency.</p>
<p>As a side note, Microsoft has said that MSF 4.0 exam plans&nbsp;will be announced in the Visual Studio 2005 release timeframe,&nbsp;mid 2005.</p>
