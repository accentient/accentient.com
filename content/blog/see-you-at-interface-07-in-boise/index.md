<p>Next week, August 2nd, hundreds of&nbsp;middle and senior-level IT Managers from SMB, corporate, academic and government organizations as well as line of business managers will be attending the 1-day forum known as <a href="http://www.f2fevents.com/about_interface.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Interface</a>. Although it's not a software/developer event, the <a href="http://www.f2fevents.com/boise07/boise_schedule.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">schedule</a> looks pretty interesting, and so does the impressive hall of <a href="http://www.f2fevents.com/boise07/boise_vendors.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">vendors</a>.</p> <p>The Interface forum runs from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM at the Boise Center on the Grove.</p>
