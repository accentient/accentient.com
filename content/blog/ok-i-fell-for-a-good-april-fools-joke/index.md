While teaching an ASP to ASP.NET migration class this week in New Jersey, I put up my buddy's Web site, to show an example of a large migration project. Kris Bloom migrated nearly 1500 .ASP pages to .ASPX pages on his site <a href="http://www.streetsourcemag.com" target="_none" rel="noopener">Street Source Magazine</a>.

Anyway, when I brought up his site for my students, I found <a href="StreetSourceMag.jpg" target="_none" rel="noopener">this page</a> instead. What made it worse, is that my server (the one hosting this blog) is about 1 meter away from Kris' servers. Needless to say, I turned white on the spot and had to stop my lecture and call Kris on the phone.

... he punk'd me! :-)
