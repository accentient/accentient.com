<p>The best tool is the simplest.&nbsp; It shouldn't get in the way of doing things, and it should be incredibly easy.&nbsp; One of my favorite Agile dev tools is the digital camera.&nbsp; Simply snap a quick photo of your whiteboard and you've got a good replacement for a $10,000 whiteboard with print / scan capabilities.&nbsp; Plus, you then have it in digital format!&nbsp; No need for scanning.</p>
<p>The problem is that you have flash reflections, etc, and that makes the image harder to read.&nbsp; You need to do some image manipulation to really make it easy to read and understand.&nbsp; And it should be in TIF or PDF format if you want to do OCR.&nbsp;&nbsp;So, head to <a href="http://www.scanr.com">www.scanr.com</a>&nbsp; This great little web site takes a digital photo of a white board, and converts it to a very nice PDF document.&nbsp; It eliminates most of the flash, rotates the image if you've taken a photo of the white board from the side, and saves it as a nice, high-quality PDF.&nbsp; Check it out!</p>
