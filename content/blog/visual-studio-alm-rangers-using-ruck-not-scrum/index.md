<p>I thought this <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/b/willy-peter_schaub/archive/2010/09/17/agile-scrum-ruck-transforming-the-rangers-ecosystem.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">post by Willy</a> was interesting. It seems that the <a href="http://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/vstudio/ee358786.aspx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Visual Studio ALM Rangers</a> are using a variation of <a href="http://www.scrum.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Scrum</a> that they are calling Ruck. <p>In the game of rugby, Ruck is a loose Scrum. The Rangers have adopted it as a term to define their unique and flexible process in order to handle distributed teams, part-time team members, and different time-zones. <p>For those of you who don’t know, the Rangers are a group of Microsoft internal and external folks (including MVPs) who build and deliver out-of-band solutions for missing features or guidance in Visual Studio.
