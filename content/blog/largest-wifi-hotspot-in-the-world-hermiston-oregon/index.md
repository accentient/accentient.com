<p>Read this in Mobile PC magazine, then did my own research. Turns out that Hermiston County, Oregon, recently installed 75 towers to transmit signals over a 600-square-mile area. This is even more amazing, if you've ever driving by or through there. I guess the horse and coyote mounted laptops will enjoy this new access!</p>
<p>There are a number of <a href="http://www.dailywireless.org/modules.php?name=News&amp;file=article&amp;sid=2036" target="none" rel="noopener">articles</a> on this. I just found it interesting.</p>
