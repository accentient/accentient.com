<p><a href="http://www.craigslist.org" target="none" rel="noopener">Craigslist</a>&nbsp;will launch its Boise site before the end of the year, according to San Francisco-based founder Craig Newmark. The site offers jobs, classifieds, personals, services,&nbsp;and (most interestingly)&nbsp;bartering! </p>
<p>Started in San Francisco in 1995, Craigslist now has Web site pages for numerous U.S. cities, plus London and several cities in Canada. Newmark, a self-proclaimed nerd, said the site initially was a way for him to find out about social events, and then share the information with others.</p>
<p>This is a nice indication that Boise is and continues to be&nbsp;a technology center, with a community of tech-minded professionals. </p>
