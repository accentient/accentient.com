<p>Wanted to let you know about&nbsp;MSBIC, which is a North Texas based non-profit organization created to build an interactive forum focused on solving business problems by leveraging Microsoft Business Intelligence technologies, as well as promoting the discipline of Business Intelligence (BI) as a whole.</p>
<p>To learn more about them, visit their <a href="http://www.msbic.com" target="none" rel="noopener">Web site</a>.</p>
