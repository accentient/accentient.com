<p>What is it, you ask?</p>
<p>CodePlex is an online software development environment for open and shared source developers to create, host and manage projects throughout the project lifecycle. It has been written from the ground up in C# using .NET 2.0 technology with <strong>Team Foundation Server</strong> on the back end. CodePlex is open to the public free of charge.</p>
