<p><a href="http://www.mydemos.com/blog/PermaLink.aspx?guid=e71faf66-f7bc-4d97-9dec-89b25102a403">Scott Duffy</a> sometimes writes a bit about VSTS.&nbsp; Not often, but enough for me to keep checking back to his blog often.&nbsp; His last post had something that just struck me.&nbsp; He puts it like this:</p>
<p>* <strong>A word about recognition</strong>: to me, recognition is simply that someone accurately understands how difficult a problem was to solve. </p>
<p>This rings true to me.&nbsp; Which is sometimes where management makes mistakes.&nbsp; Yes, it's good to congratulate someone on a job well done.&nbsp; But it means so much less coming from someone who doesn't understand the job.&nbsp; A "Wow!" under the breath of a fellow programmer, is far more recognition that a plaque handed out at the company Christmas party by someone unaware of what you really, really do.&nbsp; </p>
