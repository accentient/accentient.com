<p>Why demo your cool application to a few friends, when you can show it off to thousands of your peers at the PDC? This year's&nbsp;PDC will be the&nbsp;first-ever <em>Show Off</em> event, which is an evening event held at the PDC where you can show off your application, tip, tool, technique, animation, or anything cool that can help or inspire other developers.</p>
<p>Read more about it at <a href="http://channel9.msdn.com/ShowPost.aspx?PostID=102337" target="none" rel="noopener">Channel 9</a> or <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/mswanson/archive/2005/08/12/451034.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">Michael Swanson's</a> blog post.</p>
