<p>Just to catch you up, Microsoft's Team Foundation Server v1.0 (released March 2006) only works with WSS 2.0. WSS 2.0 is&nbsp;the old version. WSS 3.0 is the new version, and launched with Microsoft Office 2007. It's&nbsp;full of <a href="http://office.microsoft.com/en-us/sharepointtechnology/HA100738471033.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">great new features</a>, but alas TFS won't integrate with it naturally. Even installing TFS SP1 won't get you there.</p>
<p>Thanks to <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/briankel/" target="none" rel="noopener">Brian Keller</a>, a Technical Evangelist for Team System, who has published a document on <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/briankel/archive/2007/04/14/Configuring-Visual-Studio-2005-Team-Foundation-Server-with-Windows-SharePoint-Services-3.0.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">how to integrate</a> TFS with WSS 3.0. As Brian explains, keep in mind that this is a "release candidate" of guidance that&nbsp;will eventually be posted to MSDN.</p>
<p>Now I've heard of a lot of things, but an RC of a guidance document?</p>
