<p>Last night I attended the <a href="http://www.netda.net/Event/EventNewsletter.asp?EventDate=2/9/2004" target="none" rel="noopener">general meeting</a> of the <a href="http://www.netda.net/" target="none" rel="noopener">.NET Developer's Association</a>. There were two fantastic presentations.</p>
<p>The first presnetation was on RSS and syndication of blogs. It was given by Microsoft's&nbsp;most prolific blogger, <a href="http://scoble.weblogs.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Robert Scoble</a>, and the guy who designed the RSS spec, <a href="http://www.scripting.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Dave Winer</a> -- a fellow at the at Berkman Center for the Internet &amp; Society at Harvard Law School&nbsp;. I can safely say that I know a lot more about RSS and syndication now, although I'm still a bit fuzzy on how a specification based on XML can get away with <em>not</em> having a schema.</p>
<p>The second presentation was a bit canned. It&nbsp;was a scripted walkthrough of Visual Basic .NET Whidbey performed by Robert Green (a PM and the Community Lead on the Visual Basic product team). It was nice, however,&nbsp;to have access to some of the VB .NET team and see the latest builds. Unfortunately, I learned that&nbsp;the DbTable is now gone. Damn, and I just wrote an article about it! :-(</p>
