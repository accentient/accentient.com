<p>Aside from MSF, Scrum, XP, and RUP, I've been finding some&nbsp;other methodologies and frameworks as I travel and meet with teams. Some might make for interesting Process Templates in Team System. Here are just a few, in no particular order ...</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://www.prince2.org.uk/" target="none" rel="noopener">Prince2</a> -&nbsp;a process-based approach for project management providing an easily tailored and scaleable method for the management of all types of projects. The method is the de-facto standard for project management in the UK and is practiced worldwide.
<li><a href="http://www.isixsigma.com/library/content/six-sigma-newbie.asp" target="none" rel="noopener">Six Sigma</a> -&nbsp;a methodology to manage process variations that cause defects, defined as unacceptable deviation from the mean or target; and to systematically work towards managing variation to eliminate those defects
<li><a href="http://www.poppendieck.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Lean Software Development</a> - a translation of lean manufacturing principles and practices to the software development domain.
<li><a href="http://www.zifa.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Zachman Framework</a> - a framework for Enterprise Architecture which provides a formal and highly structured way of defining an enterprise's systems architecture.
<li><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DODAF" target="none" rel="noopener">DoDAF</a> - a framework for development of a systems architecture or enterprise architecture (EA).</li></ul>
<p>Added Later ...</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://www.sei.cmu.edu/tsp/tsp.html" target="none" rel="noopener">Team Software Process (TSP)</a> -&nbsp;helps a high-performance engineer to ensure quality software products, create secure software products, and improve process management in an organization</li>
<li><a href="http://www.sei.cmu.edu/tsp/psp.html" target="none" rel="noopener">Personal Software Process (PSP)</a> -&nbsp;shows engineers how to manage the quality of their projects, make commitments they can meet, improve estimating and planning, reduce defects in their products</li></ul>
<p>Do you know any others? If so, provide links in the comments.</p>
