<p><a href="http://www.cfkidaho.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Computers for Kids</a> is currently accepting computers (both working and non-working units) for refurbishment and distribution to K-14 in Idaho. Donations are tax deductible.</p> <p><a href="http://www.cfkidaho.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Computers for Kids</a> is a <a href="http://www.techsoup.org/mar" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Microsoft Authorized Refurbisher (MAR)</a>&nbsp;, which means Microsoft provides a good deal on&nbsp;Windows 2000 and sometimes Windows XP operating systems. The Community MAR program was created to increase the number of usable PCs available to non-profits, schools, and low-income families across the globe by reducing the cost of software to refurbishers. The MAR program also benefits the environment by giving new life to a significant number of computers that may otherwise be destined for landfills.</p> <p><a href="http://www.cfkidaho.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Contact Computers for Kids</a> at <a href="http://maps.live.com/default.aspx?v=2&amp;cp=43.578349~-116.290104&amp;style=r&amp;lvl=15&amp;tilt=-90&amp;dir=0&amp;alt=-1000&amp;sp=Point.r7wrk359877z_W%20Elisa%20St%2C%20Boise%2C%20ID%2083709%2C%20United%20States___&amp;encType=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">8540 W. Elisa Street, Boise, Idaho</a>&nbsp;or by calling them at (208) 345-0346.</p>
