<p>What a better way to spend Tax Day eve, than helping the (Boise) public with their variety of computer problems.</p>
<p>Boise State University&nbsp;Association of Information Technology Professionals (AITP) will be hosting&nbsp;their fifth PC Repair Fair on April 14, 2007.&nbsp; This is a community service event where we will be fixing computers for the community for free. Oh, and they&nbsp;need volunteers.</p>
<p>Saturday April 14th * 8:45am&nbsp;to 2pm (first shift) Saturday April 14th * 1:00pm&nbsp;to 7pm (second shift).</p>
<p>Learn more about the fair, including how to volunteer, <a href="http://www.abdinsights.com/aitp/repair_fair" target="none" rel="noopener">here</a>.</p>
