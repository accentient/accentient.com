<p>For those of you who joined me at <a href="http://vslive.com/2008/sf" target="_blank" rel="noopener">VSLive!</a> this week in San Francisco, I had fun sharing many worst (or un-preferred) practices I've run into over the years. My talk broke them down into several areas: TFS installation, TFS configuration, team projects, work items, and version control. Hopefully I didn't make anyone feel tool uncomfortable when I highlighted your practice on the big screen!</p> <p>Actually, it was all in good fun. By highlighting Team System worst practices, we were able to define Team System best practices and preferred practices. </p> <p>If you'd like to have a look at my slide deck, you can find it <a href="VSLive-SF2008-Worst-Practices-Slides.zip" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a> and my demo files <a href="VSLive-SF2008-Worst-Practices-Demo.zip" target="_blank" rel="noopener">here</a>.</p> <p>Feel free to let me know about any other worst or <em>worster</em> practices you may know of.</p> <p><a href="http://vslive.com/2008/sf" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><img style="border-top-width: 0px; border-left-width: 0px; border-bottom-width: 0px; border-right-width: 0px" height="119" alt="VSLiveSF" src="VSLiveSF_3.jpg" width="454" border="0"></a></p>
