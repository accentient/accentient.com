<p>Check out the dates, and register at <a href="http://register.unitedtraining.com">http://register.unitedtraining.com</a>.&nbsp; If you want a discount (and who doesn't) enter the <strong>UTNETDESK</strong> as the RSVP code!&nbsp; </p>
<p>I'll be presenting many of these, and I've got to say, the content, which I received&nbsp;last week,&nbsp;looks great!&nbsp; It promises to be very, very well worth your time.&nbsp; Plus, it's cheap!&nbsp; Two days of training for only $399 with the RSVP code (above).</p>
