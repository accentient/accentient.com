<p>&lt;mathGeek&gt; The Associated Press (AP) quotes (evidently without analysis) numbers from <a href="http://www.nationalhomeless.org/">National Coalition of the Homeless</a>.&nbsp; They claim that there are 3.5 million homeless in the US.&nbsp; Now, I don't know how many there are, and it has little to do with innumeracy.&nbsp; But they then claim that their are 3,000 deaths of homeless people every year.&nbsp; The focus of the article was on the tragedy of homeless deaths.&nbsp; To me, even one homeless person having to die alone, and on the street is too many.&nbsp; But here's where the math comes in...&nbsp; 3,000 deaths from 3.5 million homeless gives a death rate of 0.0857%.&nbsp; That seemed suspiciously low.&nbsp; A quick check of the CIA World Fact Book states that the death rate for the&nbsp;entire US is 8.34 deaths per thousand, or a rate of 0.834%.&nbsp; So, if the numbers are correct, you are 10 times LESS likely to die if you are homeless.&nbsp; </p>
<p>This seems highly unlikely to me, as homelessness seems, on first blush, to be less healthy than having a place to live.&nbsp; However, it may be that homeless people tend to be young and healthy.&nbsp; Or it could be that the at least one of the numbers given by the National Coalition of the Homeless is incorrect.&nbsp; I'm guessing that the number of deaths is fairly well documented, while the number of homeless must be determined statistically.&nbsp; If that's the case, a more likely number of homeless in the US would be around 350,000, assuming a mortality rate approximately equal to the rest of the population.&nbsp; Once again, there may be many factors that influence the actual number, but a very quick 'reality-check' on the numbers reveals the possibility of very serious numerical errors.&nbsp; (It looks like the NCH is overstating the number of homeless by 3.15 million.)&nbsp; Homelessness is a very real problem, but drastically inflating the size of the problem for attention does nobody any favors.&nbsp; Once people BS-meters are triggered, then it'2013-08-28 13:52:39's hard to take anything else said seriously, thus damaging your cause.
