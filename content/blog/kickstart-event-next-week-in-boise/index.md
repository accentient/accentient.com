This <a href="http://www.kickstartidaho.com" target="none" rel="noopener">event</a> will be held at the BSU campus next week (April 4th-6th) and wil bring together Idaho&#8217;s entrepreneurial resources in one place. The 16 individual workshops will consist of 4 tracks: Creation, Implementation, Execution, and Resources. Seminar topics include: Assessing my opportunity, Intellectual property, Marketing my business, University and Idaho National Lab resources, Presenting to Investors and many others.<br /><br /><a href="http://www.guykawasaki.com" target="none" rel="noopener">Guy Kawasaki</a> will be the keynote speaker on April 5th and there will be a full day of seminars on April 6th.
