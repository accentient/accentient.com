<p><a href="http://steven.wilssens.net/">Steven Wilssens</a> attended the preconference on Team System that Rich and I presented at PDC this year.&nbsp; He's blogged it.&nbsp; But the best is his incredible summary!&nbsp; He manages in just a few paragraphs to effectively summarize the content of the full day pre-con.&nbsp; I whole heartedly recommend his <a href="http://steven.wilssens.net/PDC05SoftwareDevelopmentWithVisualStudioTeamSystem.aspx">post</a>!&nbsp;</p>
<p>In addition, he's got several other great posts.&nbsp; I especially like his post on the&nbsp;testing "V model".&nbsp; It explains how the various classes of tests (unit, integration, acceptance,&nbsp;etc) relate to the software development lifecycle.&nbsp; You can find that post <a href="http://steven.wilssens.net/TestingLevels.aspx">here</a>.&nbsp;</p>
<p>I've gone ahead and added him to my personal blogroll.&nbsp; There's good stuff on his blog for all of you Team System addicts, especially those of you interested in testing.&nbsp; It doesn't have a lot of posts yet, but the posts it has are all very valuable.&nbsp; Check <a href="http://steven.wilssens.net/">it</a> out!</p>
