<p>Join Borland for a free educational breakfast seminar on Wednesday, November 15th, 2006 to learn more about their Software Delivery Optimization (SDO) – A Vision for Software Development. Borland has been slowly evolving into a company that&nbsp;provides&nbsp;Application Lifecycle Management tools, as well as a process consulting.</p>
<p>Specifically, they will be demonstrating</p>
<ul>
<li>Borland CaliberRM® and DefineIT® for Requirements Definition &amp; Management</li>
<li>Borland SilkCentral® Test Manager™ for Requirements-Based Testing</li>
<li>Borland StarTeam® for Configuration and Change Management </li></ul>
<p>Who should attend? Directors and Managers of: Application Development and Quality Assurance, Program and Project Managers, Architects and Business Analysts</p>
<p>The breakfast seminiar is on Wednesday, November 15, 2006 at 8:30am (until noon) at the&nbsp;Grove Hotel, 245 S. Capitol Blvd, Boise, ID 83702.</p>
<p>I'm guessing you can probably just show up, if you are interested.</p>
