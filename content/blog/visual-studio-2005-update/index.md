<p>Here are some of the points from <a href="http://blogs.msdn.com/somasegar/" target="none" rel="noopener">Soma's blog entry</a> this morning ...</p>
<ul>
<li>We're 77 days out from launch
<li>In September, there will be a Release Candidate (RC1) of Visual Studio 2005
<li>This RC will be available to MSDN subscribers, early adopters, and beta customers
<li>Beta 3 of Team Foundation Server (TFS) will be released at the same time as the RC1
<li>TFS Beta 3 will including a "Go Live" license with technical support for Premier customers
<li>TFS Beta 3 will carry us through the launch of Visual Studio 2005
<li><em>TFS RTM will be in the first quarter of 2006</em></li></ul>
<p>Please re-read that bottom bullet!<br /></p>
