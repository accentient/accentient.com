<p>I had the privilege of visiting the Delphi user group of Minneapolis, hosted by <a href="http://www.lemanix.com" target="none" rel="noopener">Lemanix</a>. The group is skippered by <a href="http://www.lemanix.com/nick/" target="none" rel="noopener">Nick Hodges</a> and Mark Theiste -- both well-known Delphi pros.</p>
<p>This is a small, but serious group. Everyone seems to be a Delphi coder, which is refreshing. The evening progressed as we watched demonstrations by a couple of the members showing off their&nbsp;coding techniques and styles, using Delphi 8.</p>
<p>We then had a discussion of Delphi 8 and it's support for .NET -- which grabbed my attention. Somehow we digressed completely into .NET and I ended up showing a short demonstration of Yukon's deep support of the CLR.</p>
<p>The <a href="http://www.mspdelphi.org" target="none" rel="noopener">group's&nbsp;site</a> is currently down, but keep an eye on it for the future.</p>
