<p>I ran across <a href="http://download.microsoft.com/download/5/0/c/50c1468e-f0e4-42c4-841f-61fdd820755a/Microsoft_Learning_64-bit_Virtualization.doc" target="_blank" rel="noopener">this document</a> today, which details out Microsoft’s virtualization strategy on implementing 64-bit (Hyper-V) virtualization in the classrooms. As virtual machines do more, and require more resources, so must the hardware/software requirements of the training centers be updated.</p> <p>In a nutshell, here are the requirements for HL6:</p> <p><u>Hardware</u> <p>&nbsp; <p>· 64-bit Intel Virtualization Technology or AMD Virtualization processor (2.8 GHz dual core or better recommended) <p>· Dual 120 GB hard disks 7200 RPM SATA or better (striped) <p>· 4 GB RAM expandable to 8 GB or higher <p>· DVD (dual layer recommended) <p>· Network adapter <p>· Sound card <p>· Video adapter aero-capable recommended <p>· Super VGA monitor (17 inch/ 43 cm) <p>&nbsp; <p><u>Software</u> <p><a name="_Toc198608997"></a>&nbsp; <p>· 64-bit Windows Server 2008 Enterprise Edition <p>· Hyper-V role configured <p>· Microsoft Learning Lab Launcher – Hyper-V version</p>
