<p>PEX = Program EXploration (a Microsoft Research <a href="http://research.microsoft.com/Pex/" target="none" rel="noopener">project</a>). I'm not really&nbsp;sure what this is, but a fellow RD, <a href="http://www.ObjectSharp.com/Barry" target="none" rel="noopener">Barry Gervin</a>, pointed it out to some of us MVPs recently after he visited a Microsoft Research "science fair".</p>
<p>Apparently, PEX automatically generates unit tests, allowing developers&nbsp;to find bugs early. In addition, it suggests to the programmer how to fix the bugs. Sounds too good to be true, but since it will&nbsp;enable "a new development experience" in Visual Studio Team System, I should probably keep an eye on it.</p>
<p>Maybe this will keep the <a href="http://codebetter.com/blogs/scott.bellware/archive/2005/11/21/134899.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">TDD purists</a> off of VSTS' back.</p>
