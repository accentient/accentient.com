<p>I'm helping my buddy <a href="http://cs.ferncrk.com/blogs/stuart" target="none" rel="noopener">Stuart Celarier</a> spread the word on this.</p>
<p><em>A Birds of a Feather session is a one-hour, moderated open discussion on any topic of great interest to conference attendees. It's not a presentation, there is no speaker or panel, there are no PowerPoint slides. Building on past successes, this year's Birds of a Feather sessions will be higher profile, taking place in the fabulous Tech&#183;Ed Cabanas.</em></p>
<p>Anyone attending Tech&#183;Ed 2005 can propose a Birds of a Feather session and everyone attending Tech&#183;Ed 2005 is encouraged to vote for the sessions you would to see happen.</p>Here's <a href="http://cs.ferncrk.com/blogs/stuart/archive/2005/02/25/171.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">Stuart's blog entry</a> with all the details.
