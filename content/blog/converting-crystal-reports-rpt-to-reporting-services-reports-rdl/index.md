<p>There are way too many <a href="http://www.businessobjects.com/products/crystal" target="none" rel="noopener">Crystal Reports</a> out there in the world and I would love to see more of them converted to Reporting Services. Therein lies the problem. I had heard that Crystal (now BusinessObjects) had blocked&nbsp;companies, like Hitachi, from building converters, because they would be&nbsp;reverse engineering (or using the APIs to reverse engineer) the .rpt files, and that would violate the license agreement.</p>
<p>Microsoft only provides migration <a href="http://msdn2.microsoft.com/en-us/library/aa964127.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">guidance</a>, but I see that one company, KTL Solutions, has a conversion <a href="http://www.ktlsolutions.com/t-crystalconverter.aspx" target="none" rel="noopener">product</a> available, and another company, Jeff-Net is offering a $25/report conversion service at <a href="http://www.rpttordl.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">rpttordl.com</a>.</p>
