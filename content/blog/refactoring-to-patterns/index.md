<p>Scott Ambler (see earlier posting) recommended this <a href="http://www.industriallogic.com/xp/refactoring" target="none" rel="noopener">soon-to-be-published book</a> by Addison-Wesley.</p>
<p>Joshua Kerievsky bridges the gap between the practice of refactoring and the art of designing with patterns. The heart of the book is a <a href="http://www.industriallogic.com/xp/refactoring/catalog.html" target="none" rel="noopener">catalog</a> of 27 refactorings that covers such areas as creation, generalization, simplification, protection and accumulation. The real-world examples in this book illustrate when it makes sense to refactor to a pattern and how low-level refactorings are combined to implement patterns. </p>
