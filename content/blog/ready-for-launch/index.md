<p>Mark your calendars! Microsoft's Boise launch of&nbsp;Windows Vista, Office 2007, and Exchange 2007 will be&nbsp;on January 30.</p>
<p>The event is broken-down into two, half-day events, one for develpers and one for IT professionals. Here are the registration links: <a href="http://msevents.microsoft.com/CUI/EventDetail.aspx?EventID=1032316008&amp;culture=en-US" target="none" rel="noopener">Developer track</a> | <a href="http://msevents.microsoft.com/CUI/EventDetail.aspx?EventID=1032316173&amp;culture=en-US" target="none" rel="noopener">IT Pro track</a>.</p>
<p>If you can't make that event, then the one in Seattle on&nbsp;February 26 might be a better choice, because it is the only launch with a keynote by Bill Gates.</p>
