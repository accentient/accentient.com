<p>Check out this article on <a href="http://money.cnn.com/2006/02/03/pf/pay_hike_jobseeker/index.htm?cnn=yes" target="none" rel="noopener">CNNMoney</a>. It lists the top 5 jobs in having&nbsp;the biggest demand for employees.&nbsp;.NET Developers made the list!</p>
<p>Here's an excerpt:</p>
<p><em>Developers who are expert users of Microsoft's software programming language .NET can make between $75,000 and $85,000 a year in major cities when they're starting out. If they pursue a job at a company that seeks someone with a background in a given field (say, a firm looking for a .NET developer experienced in using software related to derivatives) they might snag a salary hike of 15 percent or more when they switch jobs.</em><br /></p>
