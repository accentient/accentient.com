<p>A&nbsp;friend of mine from Iceland emailed&nbsp;recently. His&nbsp;tagline listed a <a href="http://www.softwarequotes.com" target="none" rel="noopener">fun Website</a> that he runs.</p>
<p>Here are a&nbsp;couple of my favorites:</p>
<ul>
<li><em>When debugging, novices insert corrective code; experts remove defective code.</em> - R. Pattis
<li><em>Programming is like sex: one mistake and you have to support it for the rest of your life.</em> - Michael Sinz
<li><em>I'd rather write programs to write programs than write programs.</em> - D. Sites
<li><em>It's hard to read through a book on the principles of magic without glancing at the cover periodically to make sure it isn't a book on software design.</em> - Bruce Tognazzini
<li><em>From a programmer's point of view, the user is a peripheral that types when you issue a read request.</em> - P. Williams</li></ul>
