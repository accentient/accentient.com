<p>As many of you may know, <a href="http://research.microsoft.com/~Gray/" target="none" rel="noopener">Dr. Jim Gray</a> (Microsoft Researcher and Turing award <a href="http://www.microsoft.com/presspass/features/1999/05-14turing.mspx" target="none" rel="noopener">recipient</a>) <a href="http://www.foxnews.com/story/0,2933,249112,00.html" target="none" rel="noopener">went missing</a> a week ago, on his sailboat Tenacious off the coast of San Francisco. I've had the pleasure, on several occasions, of speaking with Jim and learning more about his research. If we cannot find him, it will be a huge loss.</p>
