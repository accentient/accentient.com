Google debuted their <a href="http://www.google.com/webhp?complete=1&hl=en" target="none" rel="noopener">Google Suggest</a> service this week. It's still in beta, and represents the latest Google Labs accomplishment. When&nbsp;you start typing a request into the search box, a drop-down menu appears with suggestions as to what&nbsp;you might&nbsp;be looking for.
