If 2004 was the year of the blog, then <a href="http://www.smallbusinesses.blogspot.com/2005/03/is-2005-year-of-rss.html" target="none" rel="noopener">could 2005 be the year of RSS</a>? I've been hearing more and more interesting uses of RSS, so I can believe it. In fact, <a href="http://blog.coryisakson.com/">Cory Isakson</a>, even suggests that RSS might replace email altogether. That may be a stretch, but I can see his point.
