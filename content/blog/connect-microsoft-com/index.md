<p>Microsoft works when they get feedback about their software and services. Their <a href="http://connect.microsoft.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Connect</a> site&nbsp;enables this and allows you to&nbsp;participate in&nbsp;several ways, such as downloading the latest software and written material, taking surveys, exchanging ideas in newsgroup forums, and, most importantly, providing and reviewing feedback about your experiences.</p>
<p>Here are the types of resources you will find at <a href="http://connect.microsoft.com/" target="none" rel="noopener">Connect</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Announcements - New information for connection participants</li>
<li>Articles - Articles that are related to the connection</li>
<li>Surveys - Surveys can be used to collect information</li>
<li>Events - Dates for upcoming events that are related to the connection</li>
<li>Links - Web site links that relate to the connection</li>
<li>FAQs - Frequently asked questions about the connection or program</li>
<li>Downloads - Software, whitepapers, and other information</li>
<li>Product Keys - The product keys that you need to run the downloaded software</li>
<li>Newsgroups - Forums for discussing current connection issues<br /></li></ul>
